Privacy Policy

How Grand Horizon Hotel collects, stores, and protects guest data.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, place a room hold, or submit an inquiry, we store your name, email address, phone number, reservation dates, special requests, and optional campaign attribution parameters (UTM tags) in our MongoDB database.

2. Authentication and Payment Security

Account passwords are salted and hashed with PBKDF2-SHA512 before storage. Checkout payments on this site use a simulated sandbox gateway; no real credit card numbers or live banking credentials are processed or retained.

3. Cookies and First-Party Analytics

We use local storage and session storage for authentication tokens, light/dark theme preferences, and checkout timers. When you accept cookies, we record anonymous first-party page-view and reservation-funnel counts to improve booking availability and page performance.

4. Data Access and Deletion Requests

Guests may view their reservation records under My Bookings or contact our Front Desk via reservations@grandhorizon.in to request updates or removal of account data.

Terms and Conditions

Reservation rules, checkout holds, cancellation terms, and property policies.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Reservations and 10-Minute Holds

All room bookings require valid future check-in and check-out dates up to 30 consecutive nights. Starting checkout places an exclusive 10-minute hold on an assigned room. Unconfirmed holds automatically expire and release the room back to public availability.

2. Rates, Taxes, and Service Fees

Room totals include nightly base rates, applicable weekend (15% Fri-Sat) or seasonal adjustments, selected add-ons, a 12% GST hospitality tax, and a $25.00 per night resort service fee.

3. Check-In, Check-Out, and Occupancy

Standard check-in is 15:00 and check-out is 11:00. Maximum guest occupancy limits per room type are strictly enforced during booking search and check-in.

4. Cancellations and Refunds

Confirmed bookings may be cancelled before check-in via My Bookings or Front Desk staff. Valid cancellations trigger an automatic sandbox refund record for the full paid amount. Reservations already checked in must be processed as an early check-out.
